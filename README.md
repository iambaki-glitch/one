# 💖 Will You Be My Girlfriend? - Romantic Interactive Website

A romantic, playful, pink-themed website page designed to ask that special someone to be your girlfriend!

## ✨ Features Included
- **Soft Background Music**: Plays *"Home"* by Jokhay, Umair, Talha Anjum & Talha Yunus gently in the background with a floating music player pill, spinning vinyl disc animation, and manual Play/Pause control (volume preset to a soft 25%).
- **Cute SpongeBob Mascot**: An animated SpongeBob SquarePants with big sparkling blue eyes, blushing cheeks, wide happy smile, and Bikini Bottom soap bubbles, holding a glowing pulsating Valentine's heart in his hands.
- **Cuter Bubbly Font & Wave Motion**: "Will you be my girlfriend?" is styled in the bubbly, rounded `'DynaPuff'` & `'Sniglet'` font with staggered wave bounce animation on every word and an animated wiggling heart.
- **Fairy Dust Motion Trail**: An interactive sparkle trail (`✨`, `💖`, `⭐`, `🌸`, `💫`) dynamically follows her cursor and finger across the screen.
- **Floating Motion Stickers**: Animated floating love letter with wings (`💌`) and swaying heart balloons (`🎈`) float gently around the card.
- **Diamond Shimmer Motion on YES**: A diagonal gleaming light reflection sweeps continuously across the "YES" button.
- **Teleport "Poof" on NO**: Whenever the "NO" button dodges her cursor, it leaves behind a playful smoke and sparkle poof (`💨`, `✨`) at its old position before reappearing elsewhere.
- **The Grand "Yes" Celebration**:
  - Full-screen colorful confetti cannon blasts (using `canvas-confetti` + offline fallback).
  - Melodic celebratory chime using Web Audio API.
  - Ambient floating romantic hearts in the background.
  - **The Ultimate Boyfriend Resume**: Interactive cards highlighting why you are the best boyfriend ever (cuddle expert, golden fry rule, designated passenger princess chauffeur, chief bug relocator, and personal hype man).
  - **Interactive Boyfriend Perks Generator**: A button she can keep clicking to reveal more perks and sweet reasons why you're a 10/10 boyfriend!
  - **Official Sweetheart Contract**: Signed with love.
